Halo puts a small bubble on the edge of your screen. Hold it, slide your thumb to an app, and let go. That's the whole gesture.

HOW IT WORKS
• Hold the bubble to open a half-circle menu
• Your favourite apps sit on the inner ring, your recent apps on the outer ring
• Slide over an app to see its name, then let go to open it
• Let go near the bubble to cancel

MADE FOR ONE HAND
• Tap the bubble to jump back to the app you used last
• Drag the bubble anywhere; it snaps to the nearest edge
• Pick up to six favourites and hold and drag to reorder them
• Choose what a tap does, how long to hold, and whether it vibrates

FITS YOUR PHONE
• Matches your wallpaper colours on Android 12 and later
• Work profile apps can be favourites, marked with the work badge

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No internet permission: Halo cannot send anything off your phone
• No ads, no analytics, no account
• Open source: read the code at github.com/yogeshpaliyal/Halo

PERMISSIONS, EXPLAINED
• Display over other apps: draws the bubble and the menu on top of other apps
• Usage access (optional): finds your recently used apps for the outer ring. Without it, you get favourites only
• Notifications: a silent notification that keeps the bubble running, with a Stop button

Apps in Private space can't appear in Halo, because Android only shows them to the default home-screen app.
